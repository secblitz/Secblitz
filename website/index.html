How it works

  1. Check

    Secblitz looks at your computer's settings and shows which ones could be safer. It takes about a minute and changes nothing.

  2. Review

    See which settings could be safer and why it matters. Optional settings are up to you.

  3. Fix

    One click fixes everything you approved. Secblitz then confirms each fix worked.

  4. Undo

    Every change is recorded. History puts your old settings back.

Lock after wrong passwords

Leaves you open to: someone trying one password after another on your PC

What it is
Windows can pause sign-in for a few minutes after too many wrong passwords.
If it's off
Windows lets anyone keep typing passwords with no pause.
If you turn it on
Nothing you'll notice. If you mistype your password ten times, wait ten minutes.

Questions

Who is Secblitz for?

Anyone with a Windows 10 (version 22H2) or Windows 11 PC, or a computer with Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Fedora or Debian. No technical knowledge is needed. Microsoft no longer sends free security updates for Windows 10, and Secblitz cannot replace them, so Windows 11 keeps a PC safest.

Does it replace my antivirus?

No. Secblitz makes sure the protection built into Windows, including Microsoft Defender, is turned on and set up correctly. If you use a different antivirus, Secblitz doesn't change its settings.

Will it change things without asking?

No. Checking your PC changes nothing. Secblitz only makes the changes you approve, and optional settings are never selected for you.

Does Secblitz scan my network?

No. Secblitz only looks at the settings on this PC and changes them, with your approval. It does not look for other devices, list open ports, test passwords or look for ways into a PC.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Secblitz saves your original settings before changing them, and you can restore them from History. Removed apps can be restored from the Removed apps tab. Windows updates, app installs and changes made outside Secblitz can't be undone.

Could it break something?

Each fix tells you what will change, including any downside. Settings that might affect older devices or software are only offered as a choice. PCs managed by a work or school organization are left alone.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your results, history and settings stay on your PC. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Secblitz goes online only to update itself, if you left updates on, and for things you ask for, such as installing an app. With web protection on, it also downloads block lists and passes your website lookups on to the name servers your network already uses, or to Quad9. Read the full privacy policy.

How does it update?

On Windows, automatically, in the background. On Linux, Secblitz looks for a new version once a day if you turn that on, and installs it when you say yes. Every update is signed, and Secblitz verifies the signature before installing it. It never closes while you're using it and never restarts your computer.

What happens if I remove Secblitz?

You choose. Keep the safer settings Secblitz made, or put everything back the way it was before, including the apps you removed. Either way, Secblitz removes itself completely.

To remove it, open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps (on Windows 10: Apps, then Apps and features). Find Secblitz and choose Uninstall. You can also open Secblitz, go to Settings and choose Remove Secblitz from this computer. If you use the portable version, open Secblitz, go to Settings and choose Remove Secblitz from this computer to put your settings back, then delete the secblitz.exe file.

On Linux, open Secblitz, go to Settings and choose Remove Secblitz from this computer, or remove it from your software app.

Is Secblitz open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub under the MIT license.

How do I report a security problem?

Email support@secblitz.lol. Include what you found and your Secblitz version and system. We reply within a week and fix the problem before sharing details publicly.

Which languages and systems work?

Secblitz is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian, with light and dark mode. It supports Windows 10 (version 22H2) and Windows 11, including PCs with an ARM processor, and Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Fedora and Debian on Linux.

Using Linux?

Open a terminal and run this. It works on Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint and Fedora. Running it again updates Secblitz.

curl -fsSL https://secblitz.lol/install.sh | bash

On Ubuntu, which comes without curl, use this instead:

wget -qO- https://secblitz.lol/install.sh | bash
